I run design like a product team: systems first, shipped work second, and a short line between the two.

For the past decade I have led brand and design at Payoneer, building the systems a global company runs on: the website, the email design system, the brand portal, an owned photography library, and a tiered icon ecosystem. The common thread is scale. Every one of those projects had to work in dozens of markets, in the hands of people who are not designers.

Lately I have been closing the gap between design and build. I ship Chrome extensions, macOS utilities, and AI creative tools end to end, from the first Figma frame to the deployed product. It keeps me honest about what is actually buildable, and it makes the teams I lead faster.
