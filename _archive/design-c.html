03About

A design manager who still ships.

I spent 13 years leading creative teams, most recently as Design Manager at Payoneer, where I owned the brand system, the website redesign, and the tooling that let a distributed marketing organisation produce consistent work at speed.

Somewhere in there I stopped briefing tools and started building them. Now I design the interface, write the code, and ship it. That changed how I lead: I can prototype the idea in the meeting instead of describing it.

What I want next is a role where both halves count. Enough scope to shape a creative team, and enough room to keep building the production tools that make that team faster.

I work in Figma, ship in code, and design in English and Hebrew.
