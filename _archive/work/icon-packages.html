Icon packages

Icons were being drawn ad hoc for every feature and every campaign, so nothing matched anything. I led the build of one family with three tiers, functional, illustrative and animated, sharing a stroke weight, a set of proportions and a drawing style, with guidance on which tier a given job calls for.

Role
Led by me
Year
2022 to 2025
Scope
Functional UI, illustrative, animated
Shared
Stroke weight, proportion, style

What I inherited

No standard and no owner. Every feature and every campaign got its own icons, drawn to whatever weight the person in front of it preferred.

What I decided

That the fragmentation was a naming and tiering problem before it was a drawing problem. Until people knew which kind of icon a job wanted, a shared style would not hold on its own.

What I led

The tier definitions and the rules they share, the naming convention the library is filed under, and the guidance that says when to reach for each one.
