Scalable email design system

Email was the worst offender. Every team built theirs from scratch, so nothing matched and designers spent their week on production. I built a Figma template system with pre-approved components, which let marketers assemble on-brand email without a designer in the loop.

Role
Led by me
Year
2023
Scope
Figma template library
Users
Marketers, without a designer in the loop

What I inherited

Fragmented production across teams and regions, with no single source of truth.

What I decided

That the bottleneck was designers, not templates. So the system had to be usable by someone who had never opened Figma before.

What I led

The component library, the layout patterns, and the rules that keep an assembled email on brand.
