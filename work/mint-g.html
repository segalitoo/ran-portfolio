On-brand ad generator

At Payoneer I watched designers rebuild the same ad in four sizes, week after week. Mint takes a brand kit, a format and a prompt, and returns finished creative with the copy already written for the platform. The output is real layout, not a model guessing at text.

Try it →

What annoyed me

The fourth resize of an ad nobody had questioned yet. Skilled people spending their week on production rather than on the idea.

What I decided

Headless Chrome composites the final artwork, so type stays type. A model that paints a headline produces something nobody can edit afterwards.

What I built

React 19 and Vite on the front, Supabase for auth and the asset library, and Gemini generating both the visual and the platform copy inside the uploaded brand kit.

Scope

One prompt, the whole setInstagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Stories, generated together
