Icon packages

Icons were drawn fresh for every feature and campaign, so nothing matched and every new piece of work started with a redraw. I led one tiered system covering functional UI, illustrative brand and animated icons, sharing a stroke weight and a set of proportions.

What I inherited

Ad hoc icons with no shared standard. The visual language fragmented, and every new feature or campaign waited on someone drawing another set.

What I decided

Three tiers rather than one library. Functional, illustrative and animated share a stroke weight and a set of proportions, so they read as one family while each does a job the others cannot.

What I led

The system and the usage guidance that came with it, maintained alongside the brand portal from 2022 to 2025.

Scope

Three tiers, one familyFunctional, illustrative and animated
